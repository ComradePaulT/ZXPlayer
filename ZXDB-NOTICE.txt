The artwork_catalog.json database is derived from ZXDB:
https://github.com/zxdb/ZXDB

ZXDB was created and is maintained by Einar Saukas and contributors.
Source snapshot obtained 3 September 2026 from ZXDB_mysql.sql.zip.

This derived catalogue is made available under the Open Database License
(ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The full derived database is supplied as the editable artwork_catalog.json.
It selects ZX Spectrum-related titles and aliases from the source snapshot,
plus front-inlay and media-scan links with release sequence,
language and checksum metadata. It contains no image files or game data.

Image links use Spectrum Computing and the original World of Spectrum
Internet Archive mirror documented by ZXDB. The database licence does not
grant rights to the separate images; those remain with their rights holders.
