DATACORDER FASCIA WORDMARKS

The Sinclair and ZX Spectrum wordmarks included with ZXPlayer were derived
from the corresponding vector logos on Wikimedia Commons. Their file pages
identify them as public-domain text logos made from simple geometric shapes.
The names and marks may remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Sources:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sinclair.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sinclair_ZX_Spectrum-02b.svg

The graphics are included only to reproduce the appearance of the historic
ZX Spectrum +2A Datacorder fascia in this personal, non-commercial player.
